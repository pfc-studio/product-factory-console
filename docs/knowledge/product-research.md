# Product research findings

Sources: WPW Etsy market research, executive summary, competitor teardowns 00–03 and preview-image audit (13 Nov 2026; Etsy pages were mostly blocked, so many figures come from search snippets and third-party estimators: **INDEX** unless stated), Master Research KB v2 (23 Nov 2026), thumbnail competitor grid and keyword record for a weekly planner (23 Nov 2026, live pages opened), meal-planner spec research (24 Nov 2026).
Sales and revenue figures for other shops were third-party estimates and are deliberately left out. Use everything here as hypotheses for the PFC research engine, not facts.

## 1. Market shape (INDEX, 13 Nov 2026)

- Broad searches like "digital download" return over 11 million listings at a median of about US$4.
- Every niche (planners, worksheets, journal pages, wall art) is led by a few huge, low-price, mostly US or Canadian shops.
- Prices are anchored low by permanent "sales": £1–5 is normal, often shown as 50–75% off an inflated original. All-in-one digital planners appeared at under US$1.
- Demand is concentrated: in planners, the median listing gained 1 review in 30 days against 66 for the leader.
- **A new shop wins by being specific, not cheaper.**
- AI is the big trust issue after Etsy's October 2026 rule changes. None of 8 live leader listings checked carried AI disclosure wording at the time.

## 3. What to avoid

- Head-to-head generic products: "all-in-one GoodNotes planner", 600-page mega-bundles, saturated journal types, generic vintage AI art.
- AI-written instructional content (now banned) and mockup-only listings.
- Copying competitors' layouts, art or bundle names; "BESTSELLER" in titles; trademarked imagery.
- Relying on dated products alone: they expire in January. Pair with undated versions.
- Promising formats you don't deliver (a downloaded XLSX is not a live Google Sheet; a PDF is not a Canva template).
- Health, therapy, financial or educational outcome claims. Keep wellbeing content non-clinical.

## 4. Free alternatives are the real competitor

For several product types a credible free alternative exists (for example official money-planning tools, NHS meal-planning guidance and free planners). Any product must beat the free option, and the listing must not claim uniqueness, guaranteed savings or nutrition/food-safety outcomes (VERIFIED-LOG, K010; meal-planner research 24 Nov 2026). Local vocabulary alone (for example UK supermarket aisle names) is not a moat.

**PFC impact:** the research brief needs a "free alternative" field and a "why buy this instead" line.

## 5. Price ladder logic (recommendation, not observed fact)

| Tier | Example | Range |
|---|---|---|
| Entry / traffic | Single page or worksheet | £1.50–2.50 (below ~£1.50 fixed fees take too large a share) |
| Core | Themed pack | £4–7 |
| Hero | Multi-format planner or year-group pack | £9–15 |
| Mega / annual | Whole-year or all-in bundle | £18–30 |

- Use real, time-limited launch sales, never a permanent fake "70% off".
- A higher basket is not automatically more profit: compare a multi-buy against separate full-price orders (SYNTHETIC).
- Loss leaders are only worth it if they bring new buyers rather than cannibalising core products (SYNTHETIC).
- Observed close rivals for a simple undated weekly printable (11 live UK listings opened 23 Nov 2026) were priced about £1.00–£4.79, most around £1.70–£2.60 after sale discounts. A richer product priced above that range must justify it in the first image and first lines.

## 6. Buyer expectations seen in tests

- A buyer reading a 3-page weekly planner pack said "probably yes at the price IF the cut-off box is fixed and the preview shows all 3 pages", but felt it was marginal against rivals offering more pages (blind test, 23 Nov 2026, INFERENCE).
- Clarity wins: "reads instantly as a weekly planner", "per-nation bank holiday lists are clear", "A4 and Letter look like the same product" were the positive notes.
- Small grey helper text and faint keys were flagged as hard to read.

## 7. Research method lessons

- Etsy blocked direct page loads for much of the research; search-engine copies and third-party estimators filled gaps. **Label every figure observed, estimated or unknown** (PLAN §3 already requires this).
- Prices viewed from a browser located abroad showed foreign currency with VAT; record the locale with every price.
- Keyword volume tools may be unavailable on the plan in use; do not invent volumes. Use marketplace search results and market pages as proxies, labelled as such.
- Competitor listings change daily; date every grid and every price.
- Research briefs went stale fast during a month with two Etsy policy changes. PLAN's 30-day stale rule is sensible; policy facts may need a shorter re-check.
- Two research engines produced two conflicting launch lists for the same shop. A single reconciled idea backlog with scores avoids this.

## 8. Product types WPW researched or specified (generic)

Undated weekly planner (with a bonus year-at-a-glance page), reading journal, meal and shopping planner, home reset checklist, book club kit, garden journal, travel packing checklist, UK household budget (tax year), UK 2027 planner and calendar, UK primary worksheets, UK-subject wall art, visual routine cards. The undated weekly planner was the pilot and is PFC's first recipe (D-005).

Typical recipe shape that passed review: 3–12 product pages per size, plus a 5-page guide per size, as 4 buyer PDFs (product A4, product Letter, guide A4, guide Letter), no ZIP. That fits the 5-file limit with one slot spare.
