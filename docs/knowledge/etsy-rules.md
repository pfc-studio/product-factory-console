# Etsy rules relevant to PFC

Sources: WPW Master Research KB v2 (23 Nov 2026), WPW Lab "Etsy Rule Verification" v1.2 (23 Nov 2026), WPW Lab "AI disclosure line" research (23 Nov 2026), WPW competitor teardown and preview-image audit (13 Nov 2026), WPW Etsy Listing Quality Standard v1 (19 Nov 2026).
Labels: see [README](README.md). Etsy changes rules often (two major changes landed in Oct–Nov 2026). Treat every row as a dated setting.

## 1. Digital files (instant download listings)

| Rule | Value | Label | Source and date |
|---|---|---|---|
| Max files per digital listing | 5 | VERIFIED-LOG | WPW KB K004 (GPT logs, Nov 2026); Etsy Help "How to Manage Your Digital Listings" https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-gb/articles/115015628347 (opened 23 Nov 2026 for other points) |
| Max size per file | 20 MB | VERIFIED-LOG | K004; P001 audit (13 Nov 2026) |
| Buyer-visible file name | ≤ 70 characters; use letters, numbers, `.`, `_`, `-` only | VERIFIED-LOG (charset is WPW's safe subset, HOUSE) | K004 |
| Rename after upload | Not possible: file names are final at upload | VERIFIED-LOG | New Listing Checklist (19 Nov 2026); P001 audit |
| Digital variations | None. A4 and US Letter go inside the same listing as separate files | VERIFIED-LOG | K004 |
| Replacing files | Earlier buyers keep access to the replaced files | VERIFIED-LOG | K084 |
| Buyer download path | You → Purchases → Download Files on Etsy.com in a browser; guests use the receipt-email link; the Etsy app cannot download digital files | VERIFIED-LOG (app limitation wording not re-checked for 2026) | K084; Listing Standard D4 |
| Allowed types | PDF and ZIP are used by WPW and all observed rivals; Etsy's full allowed list was not re-checked | UNVERIFIED for the full list | Preview-image audit (13 Nov 2026) |

**PFC impact:** the package step must count files (≤ 5), measure bytes (< 20,000,000; aim < 15 MB for headroom), and validate names before anything is marked ready. A spare slot matters: with five files there is no room to split an oversize file.

## 2. AI use, creativity and "who made it"

| Rule | Detail | Label | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| AI disclosure | "Sellers must disclose within their listing description if an item is created with the use of AI." Disclosure goes in **each listing's description**, not only the shop announcement. | VERIFIED-1H (23 Nov 2026) | Etsy Help "What Can I Sell on Etsy?" https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360024112614 |
| Digital items must be seller-made or designed | "Digital items listed in your store must be made and/or designed by you, the seller." Seller-prompted AI creations sit under "Designed by a seller" and must disclose AI. | VERIFIED-1H (23 Nov 2026) | Same page |
| Category value for a printable | "Designed by a seller", not "Made by a seller" (that is for physical items). Never call AI work "handmade". | VERIFIED-1H | Same page; Image Requirements Help page |
| AI-generated functional instructions banned | "If you sell functional guides like patterns, recipes, or instructions, the information buyers rely on to create or use your item must be your own work, not generated by AI. This means AI-generated functional guides are no longer allowed on Etsy." | VERIFIED-1H (handbook dated 22 Oct 2026, opened 23 Nov 2026) | Etsy Seller Handbook "Important updates to AI use on Etsy" https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1522733284810 |
| Real result for instruction items | "You'll also need to show an actual result made using your instructions, not an AI-generated or simulated result." | VERIFIED-1H | Same |
| Bot listings banned | "Bot-generated listings without any human involvement aren't allowed." | VERIFIED-1H | Same |
| AI prompt bundles, compilations of others' PDFs | Not allowed (not "designed by a seller") | VERIFIED-LOG | Etsy Creativity Standards https://www.etsy.com/legal/creativity (last updated 10 Jun 2025 when read 13 Nov 2026) |
| Separate "made with AI" form control | Not confirmed on official pages. Third-party blogs claim one exists under "How it's made". | UNVERIFIED | Lab AI-disclosure research, 23 Nov 2026 |
| Enforcement | Proactive enforcement planned for early 2027; failing image rules can make the seller liable for refunds; repeat breaches can close the shop | VERIFIED-1H | Handbook article above |

**Open question (not answered by Etsy's pages):** whether a full human rewrite of an AI draft counts as "your own work". The owner's working answer (23 Nov 2026): bots draft, the owner rewrites all guide and instruction text in her own words, and photographs a filled-in printed page. PFC should record that rewrite as evidence (see proposed backlog).

**PFC impact:** marketplace profile needs `ai_disclosure_required: true` (verified 23 Nov 2026), a per-product "AI used? how?" record, a creative-input record (who wrote which text, with diffs), and a block on approving any product whose guide text is still the unedited AI draft.

## 3. Listing images and video

| Rule | Detail | Label | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Visual digital downloads | "All other digital downloads should include a visual preview or excerpt of the content buyers will receive." | VERIFIED-1H (policy last updated 5 Nov 2026) | Etsy Listing Image Requirements https://www.etsy.com/uk/legal/policy/listing-image-requirements/253962679005 |
| Accuracy | "All images must accurately reflect the item's true appearance, including colour, condition, scale, and materials." No AI edits that make an item look better or different. | VERIFIED-1H | Same |
| Unedited photo of the actual item against a real background | Stated for "most products"; digital items are named as having different requirements. Whether an ordinary printable also needs one is an interpretation, not a stated rule. WPW chose to include one (owner's photo of a filled-in printed page). | VERIFIED-1H (text) / UNVERIFIED (application to printables) | Same, plus Handbook |
| Instruction-based items | "You must include at least one real, unedited photo of something made using your product." AI images cannot be the only images of the finished result. | VERIFIED-1H | Same |
| Mockups | Print-on-demand may use stock mockups only with the stock owner's authorisation. Avoid realistic AI people in mockups (California SB 1050 from 1 Jan 2027 was cited for US buyers). | VERIFIED-1H (POD) / INDEX (SB 1050) | Same; ShopStack tracker (13 Nov 2026) |
| Photo count | Up to 20 photos and 2 videos per listing | VERIFIED-LOG | K061 |
| Photo size | At least 2000 px recommended; first photo at least 635 px on both sides; landscape or square first photo | VERIFIED-LOG | K061, Listing Standard TH1 |
| Upload weight | Uploads over 1 MB may stall | VERIFIED-LOG (Etsy warning, 19 Nov 2026) | Listing Standard P4 |
| Video | 5–15 s satisfies both Etsy pages (3–15 s and 5–15 s); MP4 under 100 MB; short side ≥ 1080 px; audio is removed; videos do not replace the unedited photo | VERIFIED-LOG | Listing Standard V2–V3; Image Requirements |

## 4. Search and listing text

| Rule | Value | Label | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Title length | ≤ 140 characters (a cap, not a target); Etsy recommends under ~15 words | VERIFIED-LOG | K040 |
| Title is fixed into the URL at first publish | Finalise before first publish | VERIFIED-LOG | Listing Standard T9 |
| Tags | 13 maximum, each ≤ 20 characters, letters, numbers and spaces (WPW also allowed `'` and `-`) | VERIFIED-LOG | K041 |
| Ranking | Holistic: title, tags, attributes, category, description and first photo; formula unknown; accurate category and attributes improve matching (categories updated Nov 2026) | VERIFIED-LOG | K042 |

| Shop sections | ≤ 20 sections, names ≤ 24 characters, one section per listing, empty sections hidden | VERIFIED-LOG | K043 |
| Shop name | ≤ 20 characters, no spaces | VERIFIED-LOG | K069 |

| Codes or links inside downloaded PDFs | No explicit Etsy rule found either way | UNVERIFIED | Lab rule verification, 23 Nov 2026 |

## 5. Bundles

Etsy native discounted bundles (VERIFIED-LOG, K025): at most 3 existing listings; the buyer must buy all of them; a listing can be in only one native bundle at a time; no stacking with sales or codes. **PFC impact:** a bundle builder must reject a listing already used in another native bundle.

## 6. Fees (UK seller) and money facts

| Fee | Rate | Label | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Listing fee | US$0.20 per listing and per renewal, even unsold (charged in USD) | VERIFIED-LOG | K020 |
| Transaction fee | 6.5% | VERIFIED-LOG | K020 |
| Payment processing (UK) | 4% + £0.20 per order, on the buyer total including tax | VERIFIED-LOG | K020 |
| Regulatory operating fee (UK) | 0.48% (an older page said 0.32%, now stale) | VERIFIED-1H (23 Nov 2026) | Etsy Help "What is a Regulatory Operating Fee" https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-gb/articles/1500011073202 |
| Offsite Ads | 15% of an attributed order (sales under US$10k in the past 365 days) or 12% above, attribution window 30 days | VERIFIED-LOG | K021 |
| VAT on seller fees | Charged on Etsy fees; unrecoverable for a seller who is not VAT-registered | VERIFIED-LOG | K020, F4 |
| Currency mismatch | Listing and payout currency mismatch can cost 2.5% conversion | VERIFIED-LOG | K028 |
| Buyer VAT on digital items | Etsy may collect and remit buyer VAT on digital items; that money is not seller income | VERIFIED-LOG | K028; Etsy Help "How VAT Works on Digital Items" |
| Payment reserves | Digital-item reserves for physical orders do not apply; a sale is not a deposit is not profit | VERIFIED-LOG | K085 |

Contribution model (synthetic, per order; P = list price in £; listing fee ≈ £0.16 proxy for US$0.20; VAT at 20% on all fees; no buyer tax on the payment base):
`fees = 0.065·P + (0.04·P + 0.20) + 0.0048·P + 0.16`, `M0 = P − 1.2·fees = 0.86824·P − 0.432`, and with a 15% Offsite Ad `M15 = M0 − 1.2·0.15·P = 0.68824·P − 0.432`.
Check: P = £4.50 gives M0 = £3.475 and M15 = £2.665, matching the WPW meal-planner worksheet (24 Nov 2026).
**Discrepancy to resolve:** the WPW research KB (K022, 23 Nov 2026) states `M0 = 0.85864·P − 0.432` and `M15 = 0.67864·P − 0.432`, which is 0.0096·P lower than the components add up to; the source of the extra 0.8% was not found. Below about £1.50 the fixed fees take too large a share either way. Re-check against a real fee invoice; never treat either formula as fact.
